Scope (MVP, high-fidelity polities):
- Production: a few sectors (agriculture, industry, services, defense, space; propose a final list), CES/Cobb-Douglas with a tech multiplier, capital accumulation, stochastic shocks, Markov regime states
- Income and wealth distribution: lognormal body + Pareto tail, about 20 quantile bins
- Tax suite applied to the bins: progressive income, wealth, corporate, VAT/sales, capital gains, estate, tariffs, subsidies; behavioral response through an elasticity of taxable income; revenue and post-tax inequality outputs
- Budget: spending lines (including defense and R&D, which feed WS-05); deficit → bonds
- Bond market: yield = f(debt/GDP, inflation, policy rate, credibility), with a nonlinear high-debt spread (start from the Millennium Dawn precedent in m1-findings §1.2); maturity structure kept minimal
- Monetary: central bank independence parameter (set by WS-04); Taylor-type rule if independent; Phillips-curve inflation
- Demographics: cohort-component model in 5-year bands; fertility and mortality as functions of income, tech, policy
- Interest groups: economic behavior (investment, labor, R&D talent) comes from WS-04 factions (P-8). Define the interface; don't duplicate it.
- Macro-tier reduced form: a few aggregates (GDP, population, debt, military capacity) for macro polities (SYS-006)
- Progressive disclosure UI data: round summary numbers plus a detailed incidence view (m1-findings §1.3)

Out of scope: detailed trade networks, financial sector, exchange-rate markets (propose the minimal FX stub needed for arms purchases), corporate stocks and bonds.

Decisions to make:
1. Sector list and production functional form
2. Monthly vs. annual update for slow variables (demographics, capital)
3. Damping and stability strategy (R-2), with the batch stability criteria the prototype must pass
4. Which variables get stochastic shocks, and their distributions
5. The interface contract with WS-04 (factions → behavior; policy → faction utility)

Working method: options → trade-offs → recommendation; at most 5 questions; tag every parameter [V]/[R]/[D].
Deliverables: spec on a branch with a PR; prototype plan (prototypes/economy/) listing the scenarios to batch-run, 1955–2060; POAM updated.
Done when: every ECO- requirement is testable, stability criteria are numeric, and the WS-04 and WS-05 interfaces are explicit.
