# WS-11 — Force Design, Manpower, Basing, Munitions & Strategic Forces · Phase A kickoff

```
Workstream WS-11 — Force Design & Basing for Orchestra (repo AbscissaOrdinate/Orchestra).

Read first: docs/conops.md (§3 force rows, §5 P-10, §6 and §6.1 force echelons, R-6, SYS-009, OQ-6), docs/research/m1-findings.md §3.3–§4, docs/specs/WS-02-architecture.md, WS-03, WS-04 and WS-05 specs (if approved), docs/specs/README.md.

Objective: produce docs/specs/WS-11-force-design.md (FRC- requirements). The level of play is strategic/operational, with War Footing as the reference point: thinner than HOI4, but with more customizable templates. No tactical layer.

Scope (MVP):
- Formation templates (P-10): a tree of sub-unit slots, equipment slots (WS-05 designs or components) and personnel; era presets as content; research unlocks slots, rules and per-service doctrine choices
- Echelons per §6.1: design from company level; tracked units are brigade/BCT, ship, squadron, SOF company, spacecraft/constellation; groupings are squadron/fleet, wing, joint task force
- Rollup: sub-units + equipment → unit aggregates (offense, defense, staying power, detection, mobility, upkeep) with breakdowns; readiness, supply and experience multipliers; WS-07 consumes only these aggregates
- Unit lifecycle: raise/commission (production lead time from WS-05 industry), refit/modernize (template version bump; partial learning reset), mothball/reserve, decommission, sell (feeds the WS-05 market)
- Manpower: pool from WS-03 demographic cohorts; conscription vs. volunteer (a WS-04 policy) and its economic cost (labor withdrawn); active/reserve/cadre tiers; mobilization lag; training pipeline
- Readiness: decays unless funded; O&S costs flow into the WS-03 budget lines
- Basing: naval, air, army, space (launch sites, ground stations) and production facilities, each with capacity, located in regions (WS-08); overseas access from WS-06 treaties; capacity limits forward deployment
- Munitions: a few aggregate classes; stockpile drawn down by combat, refilled by production
- Deployment: assign tracked units and groupings to theaters/regions with postures (defend, attack, patrol, blockade, strike, deter); supply as abstract capacity along region edges
- Strategic forces: warhead stockpile, delivery systems, survivability and posture → deterrence credibility (an input to WS-06 bargaining); nuclear use is a catastrophic escalation event, not a modeled exchange
- ISR assets: satellites, patrol aircraft, SOF reconnaissance → P-2 belief updates
- Services as WS-04 interest groups competing for budget share
- Macro-tier reduced form: military capacity aggregates only (SYS-006)

Out of scope: tactical combat; generals and leaders; tracked division/corps HQs; detailed logistics networks; nuclear exchange modeling; cyber/EW beyond a modifier; player-authored doctrine.

Decisions to make:
1. Template schema (RON) and the rollup function: keep it legible, with every aggregate explainable
2. Resolve OQ-6 with WS-02: the force block's owner and stage, and how combat losses return without breaking the one-writer rule
3. The aggregate set that WS-07 needs (agree it with the WS-07 kickoff)
4. Manpower and mobilization model: pool sizing, tier transitions, lag
5. Era preset content: how many templates per service for 1955–2060, and how research changes them

Working method: options → trade-offs → recommendation; at most 5 questions; tag values [V]/[R]/[D]. Use real-world TO&E only as inspiration for presets; the setting's forces are fictional.
Deliverables: the spec on a branch with a PR; preset template list per service and era; POAM updated.
Done when: every FRC- requirement is testable; the WS-03, WS-04, WS-05, WS-06, WS-07 and WS-08 interfaces are explicit; SYS-009 is traceable.
```
