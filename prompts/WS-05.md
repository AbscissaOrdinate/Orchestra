# WS-05 — R&D Uncertainty, Craft Design, Defense Industry & Market · Phase A kickoff

```
Workstream WS-05 — R&D, Craft Design, Defense Industry & Market for Orchestra (repo AbscissaOrdinate/Orchestra).

Read first: docs/conops.md (§3, §5 P-1/P-2, §6 R&D rows), docs/research/m1-findings.md §3, docs/specs/WS-02-architecture.md and WS-03-economy.md (if approved), docs/specs/README.md.

Objective: produce docs/specs/WS-05-rnd.md (RND- requirements) and a Python prototype plan for milestone M2(b). This system should make innovation feel uncertain and teach systems-engineering lessons.

Scope (MVP):
- World truth (P-1): for each tech, sampled at world generation: whether it works, its true performance, its true cost, and hidden enabling edges; consistent across polities for the same seed (SYS-005)
- Beliefs (P-2): a per-polity estimate ± σ, narrowed by science, testing, espionage (WS-06), and observing others
- Science vs. engineering split (Terra Invicta precedent, m1-findings §3.1): science narrows beliefs; engineering projects turn beliefs into components and designs, with execution noise
- Distributions: lognormal cost and schedule growth (start from the [V] ACAT I data in m1-findings §3.2), scaled by immaturity; beta for success probability; mixtures for multimodal outcomes
- Project slots scale with funding, with diminishing returns per project
- Hidden cross-field enablers: how they're discovered and revealed
- Craft design: hull presets + component slots + derived stats; a design is an engineering project; spec changes slip the schedule and partly reset learning (m1-findings §3.3); external component placement is cosmetic only (OQ-3 closed)
- Components carry provenance: origin polity, licensing, supply and embargo risk
- Defense industry: Wright's curve with a flattening term, production-rate effects, operations and support lifecycle costs
- Market (War Footing precedent, m1-findings §3.4): spot buy, arms deal, license, in-house; trade-offs between cost, lead time and dependency; corporations as AI suppliers
- Content: the tech graph as RON data, with procedural parameter sampling to limit hand-authoring (R-3)

Out of scope: movable-component stat effects, 3D, a detailed supply chain beyond component provenance.

Decisions to make:
1. Tech graph granularity for 1955–2060 (how many nodes? propose a count and a domain breakdown)
2. Belief representation (shared with WS-02) and the update rule (Bayesian-ish, or a simple σ decay)
3. What makes a tech a "dead end" in a given world, and how the player learns it
4. How design performance is derived from component stats (keep it simple and legible)
5. Market pricing and AI supplier behavior (minimal)

Working method: options → trade-offs → recommendation; at most 5 questions; tag values [V]/[R]/[D].
Deliverables: spec on a branch with a PR; prototype plan (prototypes/rnd/) showing belief convergence, dead-end frequency, and cost-overrun distributions over 1955–2060; POAM updated.
Done when: every RND- requirement is testable, and the WS-03 (budget lines), WS-06 (espionage) and WS-07 (design stats → combat parameters) interfaces are explicit.
```
